About Us


About Smart Library


Smart Library is a simple online platform designed to make it easier for readers to find books available in the library.

Our website allows users to explore different books, view basic information such as the book name, author and availability, and reserve a book without having to search through the entire library.


We have also included a collection of paid books where users can check the price and choose to buy or reserve a book.


Our Goal

Our goal is to make the process of finding and reserving books simple, quick and convenient. Instead of visiting the library just to check whether a particular book is available, users can first check the collection through our website.

What We Offer

  • 📚 Browse available books
  • 🔍 Search for a particular book
  • ℹ️ View book details
  • 📖 Check book availability
  • 📝 Reserve books
  • 💰 Explore books available for purchase
